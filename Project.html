01 / PROJECT

THE MISSION

Creating packaging that can continue serving a purpose after it reaches the Moon.

THE PROBLEM

What happens to packaging after the supplies are removed?

Future lunar missions will require astronauts to transport large amounts of equipment and supplies. Packaging protects these materials during transportation, but once the supplies are removed, the packaging can become another item that must be managed.

LunarLink Locker explores a different approach: designing the packaging so that it can become something useful after its original purpose is complete.

OUR SOLUTION

Pack it.
Unpack it.
Reuse it.

01

TRANSPORT

The locker protects supplies during transportation to the lunar environment.

02

DISASSEMBLE

Quick-release connections allow the components to be separated after unpacking.

03

REASSEMBLE

The components can be configured into useful lunar infrastructure.

PROJECT GOALS

What we're trying to achieve

01 / DURABILITY

Create a structure capable of protecting its contents during handling and transportation.

02 / REUSABILITY

Give the locker a second purpose after the supplies have been removed.

03 / SIMPLICITY

Use a practical assembly system that can be handled and reconfigured efficiently.

04 / EFFICIENCY

Combine transportation and infrastructure into one multipurpose system.
