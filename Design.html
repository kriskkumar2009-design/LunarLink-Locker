REUSE CONCEPT

From locker to table

Once the locker has completed its transportation purpose, its components can be disassembled and rearranged into useful infrastructure.

Our current concept focuses on creating a table that could provide astronauts with an additional functional surface without requiring an entirely new structure to be transported.
