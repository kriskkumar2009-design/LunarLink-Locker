LunarLink Locker

A multi-purpose lunar locker designed to store supplies and be reused as a table.

Learn About Our Project

What Is LunarLink Locker?

LunarLink Locker is a reusable storage system made from connected panels. After the locker is no longer needed for storage, the panels can be taken apart and reused to make a table.

📦 Store

The locker is designed to hold and organize supplies.

🔧 Connect

The panels connect together to create the locker structure.

🪑 Reuse

The panels can be taken apart and reused to create a table.

From Locker to Table

Our design gives the panels another purpose after the locker is taken apart. The pieces can be connected together to create a table.
