One Locker. Multiple Purposes.

Instead of becoming waste after delivery, the locker can be taken apart and reused for other things.

1. Pack

Supplies stay protected and organized during transportation.

2. Unpack

The locker comes apart using removable connections.

3. Reuse

The parts become useful equipment after delivery.
