THE IDEA

One Locker. Multiple Purposes.

LunarLink Locker is designed to make the most out of packaging used during lunar missions. Instead of becoming waste after delivery, the locker can be taken apart and reused for other purposes.

01

PACK

Supplies are protected and organized inside the locker during transportation.

02

UNPACK

The locker can be assembled and disassembled using removable connections.

03

REUSE

After delivery, the parts can be reused to create useful equipment.
