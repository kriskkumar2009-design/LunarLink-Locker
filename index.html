LunarLink Locker

A multi-purpose lunar storage locker designed to hold supplies and give its panels another purpose after storage is no longer needed.

Our Project Explore the Design →

LunarLink Locker concept

THE IDEA

Storage That Can Be Reused

Our team is working on a locker made from connected panels. Instead of leaving the panels unused after the locker is taken apart, they can be assembled into a table.

📦

Store Supplies

Keep supplies organized inside a multi-purpose storage locker.

🔧

Connect Panels

Use connecting parts to assemble the locker structure.

♻️

Reuse Materials

Take the locker apart and reuse its panels to make a table.

HOW IT WORKS

From Locker to Table

The main idea is to make the locker useful for more than one purpose. The panels can be disconnected and arranged in a new way.

01

Assemble the Locker

Connect the panels to create a place for storing supplies.

02

Separate the Panels

Remove the connections so the panels can be rearranged.

03

Build a Table

Reconnect the panels in a different arrangement to create a table.

CURRENT PROGRESS

Building Our Model
