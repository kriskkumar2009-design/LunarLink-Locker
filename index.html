LunarLink Locker

A multi-purpose packing box designed for future lunar missions.

Learn About Our Project

What Is LunarLink Locker?

LunarLink Locker is our NASA HUNCH Design and Prototype project. Our goal is to create useful packaging for supplies sent to the Moon.

Instead of becoming waste after the supplies are removed, the locker can be taken apart and reused for another purpose.

Our Project

1/3 Scale

Our prototype is built at one-third scale.

Reusable

The parts can be reused after the box is unpacked.

NASA HUNCH

This project is part of the NASA HUNCH program.

From Box to Table

Our current reuse concept is a small table made from the locker components.
