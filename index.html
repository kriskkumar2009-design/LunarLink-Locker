LOCKER

Multi-Purpose Lunar Locker

A reusable lunar packing system designed to transport supplies and give its materials a second purpose on the Moon.

Explore Project View Design

THE CONCEPT

One Locker.
Multiple Purposes.

LunarLink Locker is our approach to the NASA HUNCH Multi-Purpose Lunar Locker challenge.

The locker is designed to carry supplies to the Moon. After those supplies are removed, the locker can be taken apart and its parts reused instead of becoming unnecessary packing waste.

01

PACK

Supplies are transported safely inside the locker.

02

UNPACK

The supplies are removed and the locker is disassembled.

03

REUSE

The parts are reassembled into something useful for a lunar base.

OUR PROTOTYPE

Designed for the Moon

Our team is developing a 1/3-scale prototype that demonstrates how a lunar packing locker can be reused after its original purpose is complete.

AFTER UNPACKING

From packing box
to table.

Our current reuse concept transforms the locker components into a small table. Because our prototype is built at 1/3 scale, the table is also demonstrated at a smaller scale.

The goal is to show how the same parts used to transport supplies can have another useful purpose on the lunar surface.
