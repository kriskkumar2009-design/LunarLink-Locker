Our Project

Built for the Moon.

LunarLink Locker is a packing box for future lunar missions. It stays useful even after the supplies are removed.

Why Do We Need It?

Supplies sent to the Moon need protection. Instead of packaging that is used once, we are making a structure with another purpose after delivery.

Protect

Keep supplies organized and safe during transportation.

Connect

Removable connections make it easy to put together and take apart.

Reuse

Give the materials another purpose after unpacking.
