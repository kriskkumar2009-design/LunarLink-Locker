OUR PROJECT

Built for the Moon.

LunarLink Locker is a multi-purpose packing box designed for future lunar missions. The goal is to make the packaging useful even after the supplies have been removed.

THE CHALLENGE

Why Do We Need It?

Supplies sent to the Moon need to be protected during transportation. Instead of creating packaging that is only used once, our project focuses on creating a structure that can have another purpose after delivery.

01

PROTECT

Keep supplies organized and protected during transportation.

02

CONNECT

Use removable connections to make the locker easier to assemble and take apart.

03

REUSE

Give the materials another purpose after the locker is unpacked.
