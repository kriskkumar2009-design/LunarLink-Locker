Our Project

A Locker With a Purpose.

LunarLink Locker is a storage system designed to have another use after it is no longer needed for storage.

The Idea

We wanted to create a locker that would not simply become unused after the supplies were removed. Our idea allows the locker panels to be taken apart and reused.

📦 Storage

The locker provides a place to organize and store supplies.

🔗 Panels

The locker is made from panels that connect together.

♻️ Reuse

The panels can be reused after the locker is taken apart.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a simple design that can work as a storage locker and later be turned into a useful table.
