Our Design

From Locker to Table.

Our design uses connected panels that can be assembled as a locker and later reused as a table.

How It Works

The locker is made from panels that connect together. When the locker is no longer needed, the panels can be separated and rearranged.

1. Connect

The panels are connected together to create the locker.

2. Take Apart

The panels can be separated from the locker.

3. Build a Table

The panels can be rearranged to create a table.

Panel Connections

Our design uses removable connections to hold the panels together. We have considered pins, clips, and latches for connecting the parts.
