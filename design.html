Our Design

Designed to Be Reused.

A strong, stackable structure that can be assembled, taken apart, and reused.

How It Works

The locker is made from connected panels. Removable pins, clips, or connections let us take it apart and use the pieces for something new.

Stackable

The rectangular shape lets multiple lockers stack.

Removable

Panels separate using pins, clips, or connections.

Reusable

Parts can be rearranged into another useful structure.

Our Current Concept

Our prototype shows how the locker can be assembled and then reused as a table.
