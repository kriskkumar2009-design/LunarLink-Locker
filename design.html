OUR DESIGN

Designed to
Be Reused.

Our design focuses on creating a strong, stackable structure that can be assembled, disassembled, and reused.

DESIGN FEATURES

How It Works

The locker is made from connected panels. The connections allow the structure to be taken apart and used for another purpose after unpacking.

01

STACKABLE

The rectangular shape allows multiple lockers to be stacked.

02

REMOVABLE

Panels can be separated using removable pins, clips, or connections.

03

REUSABLE

The parts can be rearranged into another useful structure.

PROTOTYPE

Our Current Concept

Our current prototype demonstrates how the locker can be assembled and reused as a table.

TABLE REUSE

After the locker has completed its main purpose, the structure can be transformed into a table.
