Our Design

Designed to Be Reused.

Our design uses connected panels that can be assembled, taken apart, and rearranged.

How It Works

The panels connect together to create the locker. They can later be separated and arranged into different structures.

🧩 Stackable

The panels can be stacked and connected together.

🔧 Removable

Pins, clips, or latches can allow the panels to be taken apart.

🌙 Reusable

The panels can be rearranged into different structures.
