Our Progress

This page shows the progress our team has made throughout the NASA HUNCH project.

Project Timeline

We documented our work throughout the project, from developing our idea to creating our current 3D-printed model.

August

We began working on the project and started developing ideas for the LunarLink Locker.

September

We continued developing the design and worked on the CAD model. On September 25, we learned that the materials for the full prototype were unavailable.

October

We continued working on the design and moved forward with a 3D-printed model of the LunarLink Locker.

Important Project Update

September 25

We found out that we would not receive the materials needed for the full prototype. Because of this, our team changed our plan and decided to create a 3D-printed model instead.
