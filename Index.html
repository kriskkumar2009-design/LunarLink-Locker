Explore Project View Design

THE CONCEPT

One Locker.
Multiple Purposes.

LunarLink Locker is our response to the challenge of creating useful packaging for future lunar missions.

Instead of treating packaging as waste after supplies are removed, our design allows the locker to be disassembled and reused as lunar infrastructure.

01

PACK

Supplies are safely transported inside the locker.

02

UNPACK

The locker is opened and its components are separated.

03

REUSE

Components are reassembled into useful infrastructure.

AFTER UNPACKING

Packaging becomes infrastructure.

Our current reuse concept transforms the locker components into a functional table. This gives packaging a second purpose instead of requiring astronauts to dispose of it.
